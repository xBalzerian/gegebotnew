## UNIQUE BOT PERSONALITIES

### WriteBot (Content Writer)
- **Visual**: Floating quill pen, papers, coffee cup with steam
- **Animation**: Papers float around, quill bobs, steam rises
- **Banter**: "Coffee's brewing, creativity flowing ☕"
- **Hover**: Papers shuffle excitedly

### PixelBot (Image Generation)  
- **Visual**: Color palette, paintbrush, swirling color blobs
- **Animation**: Colors swirl and pulse, brush rotates
- **Banter**: "Mixing the perfect palette... 🎨"
- **Hover**: Colors intensify

### KeyBot (Keyword Mapper)
- **Visual**: Grid background, data nodes, connection lines
- **Animation**: Nodes pulse, data flows along lines
- **Banter**: "Analyzing patterns... fascinating 📊"
- **Hover**: Magnifying glass effect

### PressBot (WordPress Publisher)
- **Visual**: Rocket, newspaper, globe
- **Animation**: Rocket hovers, exhaust particles, globe spins
- **Banter**: "Ready to launch! 🚀"
- **Hover**: Rocket engine revs

## ANIMATION STATES

| State | WriteBot | PixelBot | KeyBot | PressBot |
|-------|----------|----------|--------|----------|
| **Idle** | Gentle bob, papers float | Colors swirl slowly | Nodes pulse slowly | Rocket hovers |
| **Hover** | Papers shuffle, quill points | Colors intensify | Nodes highlight | Engine revs |
| **Working** | Rapid scribbling | Brush painting | Rapid connections | Rocket launching |
| **Done** | Papers stack, bow | Canvas sparkle | Network lights up | Confetti burst |
